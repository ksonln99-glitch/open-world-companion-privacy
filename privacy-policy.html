Privacy Policy

Open World Companion
Effective date: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Open World Companion mobile application (the “App”) handles information. The App is an independently developed, fan-made companion/reference app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any game publisher or developer.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores certain preferences and app state locally on your device using Android shared preferences. This may include your language preference, map/favorite selections, progress or garage selections, Premium entitlement state, and the time an app-open advertisement was last shown. This information is used to provide the App’s features. The current App implementation does not send this locally stored information to an Open World Companion server. The App does not provide user accounts or cloud synchronization.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The App uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to request and display banner and app-open advertisements. Google’s SDK may automatically collect and share information such as your IP address, app interactions (for example, app launches, taps and ad views), diagnostic information, and device or advertising identifiers. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security and fraud prevention. IP address information may be used to estimate a device’s general location.

Information handled by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is transmitted using encryption in transit (TLS), as described by Google. Google’s processing is also governed by Google’s own privacy policies and terms. For details, see Google Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

You can manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google account settings. Ad availability and personalization may depend on your location, applicable consent requirements, and your settings.

3. In-app purchases

The App offers a one-time Premium purchase through Google Play Billing. Payment details, such as card information, are handled by Google Play; the App does not receive or store your full payment card details. The App receives purchase-related information from Google Play, such as the purchased product and purchase state, to activate or restore Premium. The App stores the Premium entitlement locally on your device. Google Play processes purchases under its own terms and privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy.

4. Information we do not request directly

The current App manifest does not request access to precise device location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files. The App does not include an account registration feature. The App does require internet access to load advertisements and communicate with Google Play services.

5. Data sharing

The App developer does not operate a backend server for user profiles or app progress in the current version. Information may be processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing as described above. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored App preferences and progress remain on your device until you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Uninstalling or clearing storage may remove locally stored progress and preferences. For questions or requests relating to information handled directly by the App developer, contact us using the email below. Information processed by Google is subject to Google’s retention and deletion controls and policies.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not designed specifically for children and is not intended to solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the App developer, please contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App’s features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
